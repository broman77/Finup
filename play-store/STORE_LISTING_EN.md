# Google Play — store listing (EN)

## App name
SaldoNest — Money Tracker

## Short description
Track income, expenses, accounts, categories and reports without an account.

## Full description
SaldoNest is a simple local-first personal finance tracker.

Add income and expenses, organize transactions with your own categories and accounts, monitor balances, and review spending by month, year, or a custom period.

Features:
• income and expense tracking;
• multiple accounts and total balance;
• custom income and expense categories;
• edit and delete transactions;
• monthly, yearly and custom-period reports;
• charts and spending breakdowns;
• Excel export;
• JSON backup and restore on another device;
• light, dark and system themes;
• selectable interface accent colors.

Your finance records are stored locally on your device. No account is required, and the app does not send your financial records to the developer. Backup and export files are created only when you choose to create them.

SaldoNest is not a bank, payment service, investment service, or financial adviser. It is a manual personal finance tracking tool.
